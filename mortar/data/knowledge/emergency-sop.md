---
title: Emergency maintenance SOP
scope: global
---
Northwind Residential standard operating procedure for after-hours and emergency maintenance.
Priorities: P1 emergency (life safety or active property damage — respond immediately, 24/7),
P2 urgent (habitability or risk of damage — respond within 24 hours), P3 routine (schedule in business hours).

## Active water intrusion
Water coming through a ceiling, wall or light fixture, a burst pipe, or flooding is P1.
Resident steps: close the unit shutoff valve under the kitchen sink; keep away from wet outlets and fixtures and switch off that room's breaker only if it can be reached safely;
move valuables, put down towels or a bucket, and take photos.
Dispatch a 24/7 plumber immediately. In stacked buildings, contact the unit above — it is the most likely source.
Water mitigation (drying) is scheduled once the source is stopped and the plumber has assessed the damage.

## Gas smell or carbon monoxide alarm
P1. Residents must leave the unit immediately, avoid switches and flames, and call 911 and the gas utility from outside.
The agent never gives troubleshooting steps for gas or CO — it directs residents to safety and pages the on-call manager.

## Fire, smoke or electrical sparks
P1. Residents call 911 first. Electrical burning smells, sparking outlets or a hot breaker panel: switch off the breaker only if it is safe, then leave the room.
Dispatch a licensed electrician after emergency services have cleared the unit.

## Sewage backup
P1 when sewage is entering the unit. Residents stop using all water fixtures. Dispatch a plumber; schedule sanitation.

## No heat or no air conditioning
No heat is P1 when the outside temperature is at or below 40°F, otherwise P2.
No air conditioning is P1 when the outside temperature is at or above 95°F or a resident is medically vulnerable, otherwise P2.

## Lockouts
After-hours lockouts are P2. Identity must be verified by the on-call manager before a locksmith is dispatched.

## Spending authority
Vendors may be dispatched for emergencies up to the owner's pre-approved limit. Estimates above the limit need owner approval before work proceeds,
except for the minimum work needed to stop active damage.
