Northwind Residential leasing policy. These rules apply identically to every applicant and every listing.

## Fair housing
We follow the Fair Housing Act and Texas fair housing law. We never steer, discourage, or describe a home or neighborhood in terms of
race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
Questions about neighborhood safety, demographics or "who lives there" are answered with objective public resources, never opinions.

## Screening criteria
Gross household income of at least 3 times the monthly rent. Credit score of 620 or higher, or a larger deposit.
No evictions in the past 5 years. Every adult (18+) applies and is screened.

## Fees and holding
Application fee $55 per adult. A holding deposit equal to one week of rent reserves a home for up to 7 days after approval
and is applied to the security deposit.

## Concessions and pricing
Rent and concessions are set by the leasing manager. Agents may not offer discounts, waive fees or promise a price — requests go to the leasing manager.

## Touring and response times
Inquiries receive a response within 5 minutes, 24/7. Self-tours run 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily; agent-led tours on request in business hours.
