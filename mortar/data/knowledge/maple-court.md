Maple Court, 4100 Maple Ave, Austin TX 78751. 12-unit, three-storey walk-up built in 1986.
Units are stacked: 1A → 2A → 3A and 1B → 2B → 3B (water from a leak travels straight down the stack).

## Water shutoff valves
Each unit has its own shutoff valve under the kitchen sink (turn clockwise to close).
Toilets and the bathroom vanity also have individual angle stops behind or under the fixture.
The building main shutoff is in the basement utility room behind door B (key in the maintenance lockbox).
Closing the building main cuts water to all 12 units — only do this if a unit valve cannot stop the leak.

## Electrical panels and breakers
Each unit's breaker panel is in the hallway closet next to the bathroom.
Bathroom and kitchen circuits are labelled. If water is near outlets or light fixtures,
residents should switch off that room's breaker only if they can reach the panel without touching water.

## Plumbing history
Supply lines in the B-stack bathrooms (1B, 2B, 3B) are original braided lines installed in 2009 — past their typical life.
A-stack supply lines were replaced in 2024. Water heaters are individual 40-gallon tanks in each unit's utility closet.

## Access and entry
Vendor access is through the maintenance lockbox at the side gate; the code is issued by the on-call manager per visit and is never sent by the agent.
Emergency entry into an occupied unit requires approval from the on-call manager and written notice to the resident.

## Trash, parking and amenities
Trash and recycling pickup is Tuesday and Friday. Covered parking is assigned; visitor spaces are by the leasing office.
