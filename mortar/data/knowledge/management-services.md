What Northwind Residential offers to owners of single-family and small multifamily rentals in the Austin metro.

## Fees
Management fee: 8% of collected rent, with no fee while a home is vacant.
Leasing fee: 50% of the first month's rent when we place a new resident. Lease renewal fee: $250.
No setup fee and no hidden markup on maintenance.

## What is included
Marketing and syndication to major listing sites, self-tour showings, screening, leases, rent collection, 24/7 maintenance response,
vendor coordination, inspections, and monthly owner statements with an online owner portal.

## Maintenance and owner approvals
Owners set a pre-approved spending limit (most choose $500 to $1,000). Emergencies are handled immediately within that limit;
anything above it is sent to the owner for approval with photos and the vendor's estimate.

## Vacancy and pricing
Rents are priced with current comparable listings. Average days to lease across our portfolio was 21 days over the last 12 months.

## Contracts
Management agreements are month-to-month after the first 6 months, with 30 days' notice to cancel and no cancellation fee.
