# ShopEase — 30 Social Media Post Designs

A complete, ready-to-produce social content package for **ShopEase**, a Pakistani digital solutions company offering custom MERN-stack web development, SEO & content strategy, and Easypaisa/JazzCash payment integration.

Every post includes: **(1)** a visual layout spec (template, colors, composition, imagery), **(2)** the exact ready-to-publish caption, and **(3)** the recommended platform and posting context. All designs share one brand system so the feed reads as a single, professional identity.

## Suggested 10-Week Posting Calendar

Three posts per week, rotating pillars so no week is all-sales and no week is all-education:

- **Weeks 1–2:** Posts 1, 7, 13 · 2, 8, 25 — anchor statements + first carousels + first CTA
- **Weeks 3–4:** Posts 3, 9, 14 · 4, 10, 26 — stats + checklists + audit offer
- **Weeks 5–6:** Posts 5, 11, 15 · 6, 12, 16 — depth on all three service pillars
- **Weeks 7–8:** Posts 19, 20, 17 · 21, 22, 27 — success stories + seasonal urgency
- **Weeks 9–10:** Posts 23, 18, 28 · 24, 29, 30 — proof, recap, founder close

Rules of thumb: LinkedIn Tue–Thu 9–11 a.m. PKT · Instagram/Facebook 7–9 p.m. PKT · always reply to comments within the first hour · pin Post 29 on every profile.
