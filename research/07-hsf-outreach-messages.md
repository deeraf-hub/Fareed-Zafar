# Outreach copy: 26 message sets for the healthcare, SaaS and fintech list

Written for rows in `06-healthcare-saas-fintech.md`. Two halves, because these prospects need two completely different things said to them.

**Part 1, rows with a live posting.** These are applications, not cold pitches. The reader has already decided they need this work. Your only job is to be the one they open first. Nobody here needs convincing that decks matter, so every sentence spent explaining that is a sentence wasted.

**Part 2, rows with no posting.** These are cold LinkedIn notes, each under 300 characters because that is the connection-request limit. The reader has not decided anything. Your job is to make them realise they have a problem they had not named yet.

## Before you send anything

Three placeholders appear in Part 1. Fill them in, do not send them as they are.

- `[RATE]` your hourly figure. Six of these companies publish theirs, which is listed in `06-healthcare-saas-fintech.md`. Price against it.
- `[HOURS]` how many hours a week you can genuinely commit.
- `[LINK]` the specific portfolio piece, not your homepage. One link, chosen for that reader.

Two rules that have not changed. Verify the posting is still live before you reference it, and never use a first name you have not seen on a real profile.

## What is deliberately absent from this copy

No greeting about hoping they are well. No sentence that starts with what you do for a living. No compliment followed by a pitch. No em-dashes. Nothing that could be pasted to a second company without changing a word.

Each message also ends with a question that takes under ten seconds to answer, because a question that needs thought gets answered tomorrow, which means never.

# Sequencing, so this does not become a full-time job

Twenty-six messages is about three weeks of real outreach, not one afternoon. Order matters more than volume.

**Week one.** Send the six Part 1 applications where the posting names your exact work: WITHIN, Nexxen, Avalere Health, Vue Health, Spectrum Science, 100PitchDecks. These have the highest reply rate on the list, and one yes changes what you need from the rest of it.

**Week two.** The remaining Part 1 applications, plus the two rebrands with the shortest fuse: Skyro, because it is three days old, and Dana-Farber, because a fundraising calendar does not slip.

**Week three.** The funded rows. Do the two-minute check on each one first, careers page and LinkedIn, and delete any that already has a Head of Brand. Expect to delete several, and count that as the research working rather than failing.

**Do not** send the funded-company notes before the applications. Applications reply in days. Cold notes reply in weeks, and only sometimes. Get the fast ones moving first.

# One thing worth more than any single message here

Five of these prospects have told you their rate in public: $75 to $90 at Avalere Health, $55 at Syneos, $40 to $60 at the New York medical center, up to $40 at Toloka, and $125 to $200 at Flex for contract design.

That spread is not about skill. It is about who is buying. The agency roster pays the least and the direct client pays the most, for work that is often identical. Keep that in view when you decide where the next month goes.
