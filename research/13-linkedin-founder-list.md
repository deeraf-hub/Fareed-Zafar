# Round five: 72 founders of small companies, ranked by reply odds

Researched 1 and 2 October 2026. Rows 275 to 346, continuing from file 11.

## How the list was built

- Every person has a dated reason to need a deck now: a funding round, an FDA decision, a product launch or clinic opening between June and September 2026, or an accelerator Demo Day still ahead.
- Each person was checked by two separate agents. One verified the facts; the other tried to prove them wrong (wrong person, wrong date, company too big). Nobody was disproved. 12 candidates were dropped (listed at the end).
- "Last own post" comes from Google's index of their LinkedIn posts, counted only when the person wrote the post. Ages are as of 1 Oct 2026.
- Company size and in-house design staff were checked where possible. Anything that lowers the odds is in "Watch out".
- Nobody from rounds one to four is repeated, and nobody is based in Germany, Austria or Italy.

## Tiers

- **Tier 1 (19 people):** verified, small, and posted on LinkedIn in the last five weeks. Message these first.
- **Tier 2 (23 people):** verified, but no recent post of their own was found, or there is a caveat. The last three are lower priority.
- **Tier 3 (30 people):** found in the final pass. Funding and profile are mostly confirmed, but posting activity was not checked because the search tools ran out. Open their profile before messaging. The last six are weak.
