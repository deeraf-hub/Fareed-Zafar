# LinkedIn executive list: healthcare first, other sectors second

**Round four, researched 30 September 2026.** Rows 224 to 274.

51 named executives: 38 healthcare CEOs, 5 CEOs from other sectors, and 8 senior leaders who are the better door at the larger companies. Every one of them was checked three ways:

1. **The person and title** come from a 2026 press release, funding announcement or news story, not from memory.
2. **The LinkedIn URL** is the profile that came back in search results for that name and company. None were guessed or built from a pattern.
3. **The last-post date** is decoded from the ID of their most recent personal post that search could find. LinkedIn post IDs carry a timestamp, so this is a real date, not an estimate. It is a floor: they may have posted since, but they posted at least this recently.

The dates cross-check. Katie Diasti's latest post is dated 9 September, the day Viv announced its round. Navin Nagiah's is 17 February, the day Daffodil announced its Series A. Jack O'Hara's is 11 March, the day of Translucent's Series A.

---

## How to read the list

**Trigger** is why this person needs decks right now. Someone who just raised has a board to report to, a new market to sell into, and the next round starting in 12 to 18 months. A new CEO needs their own story told their own way.

**Last post** tells you whether a LinkedIn DM will get read:

| Last post | What it means | How to reach them |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 | Active. They are in their feed. | LinkedIn connection note |
| 2025 | Posts now and then. | LinkedIn note, then a comment on their next post |
| 2024 or older, or none found | Rarely on LinkedIn. | Use the senior contact in Section E, or a warm intro |

**Sweet spot:** seed to Series B, and any new CEO. Past that, most companies have a brand team and the CEO won't pick the designer. For those, pitch the person in Section E instead.
