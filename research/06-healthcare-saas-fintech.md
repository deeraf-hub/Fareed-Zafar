# Healthcare, SaaS and Fintech: 82 researched prospects

Researched 18 September 2026. United States first, then UK, Europe, Singapore, Canada and the Gulf.

This list continues the numbering from `02-prospect-list.md`, so rows here run 101 to 182. Nothing on it repeats a company from the first hundred.

---

## How this list was built, and why it is ordered this way

The first round taught one lesson worth more than the other ninety-nine: **the size of a company tells you nothing about whether it will pay you.** Twenty-eight of the biggest names on the earlier list turned out to have in-house studios doing exactly the work being pitched. The ones that converted on paper were smaller, and all of them shared a single trait. There was public proof they already pay outside designers.

So this round is sorted by proof of purchase, not by logo.

| Tier | What the evidence is | Rows | Why it sits here |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **1** | A live posting for a freelance or contract designer that names decks, presentations, brand or collateral work | 33 | They have a budget, an approval process and a gap. You are answering a question they already asked out loud. |
| **2** | An agency or studio that runs a standing freelance roster | 13 | Their product is the deliverable. Overflow is structural, not seasonal. |
| **3** | A rebrand shipped or in flight | 9 | A new identity creates months of collateral that nobody staffed for. |
| **4** | Funded recently, with no in-house creative team findable | 22 | Money landed, the team has not been hired yet. Timing is everything and it is short. |
| **5** | Sector events, played from the sponsor side | 5 | Do not sell to the organiser. Sell to the 950 companies buying booths. |

Tier 1 is where the next four weeks should go. Tier 4 is where the money is biggest and the odds are thinnest.

---

## Read the evidence column before you write a word

| Grade | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | A specific live posting or company announcement, quoted, with a rate or a scope. Safe to reference directly. |
| **Medium** | The funding round or the company fact is confirmed. Whether they have an in-house designer is not. Ask, do not assume. |
| **Weak** | The company appears in a sector roundup and nothing more. Research it yourself before you spend a message on it. |

One honest warning that applies to every row. **Job postings rot.** Everything in Tier 1 was live in search results on 18 September 2026. Some will be filled by the time you read it. Check before you reference a posting by name, because nothing kills a cold message faster than describing a role that closed three weeks ago.

# Tier 3. Rebrands in flight

They discover it.

The right moment to write is four to ten weeks after the launch announcement, when the gap between the new brand and the old collateral has become somebody's problem.

# Tier 4. Funded recently, in-house creative not yet visible

Highest budgets and worst odds on this list, in the same rows.

Read this tier honestly. A confirmed funding round is not a confirmed need. What the money buys is a window: for roughly one to two quarters after a raise there is cash, a mandate to look bigger than they are, a board that now expects quarterly reporting decks, and usually no brand designer yet. After that they hire someone and the window shuts.

Two practical filters before you spend a message here. Check their careers page for any design role, and check whether a Head of Brand or Head of Marketing already exists on LinkedIn. If either is true, move on. The evidence grade below covers the funding, not the creative-team question, and that question is yours to answer.

# Gaps, stated as gaps

These are holes in the research, not negative findings. An unverified row is not a disqualified one.

1. **No individual was named as a contact.** Same constraint as the first round. This environment's egress policy blocks LinkedIn, so no profile was opened or verified. Every row gives a role to target instead of a person. Confirm the name yourself before you write.

2. **No job posting was fetched directly.** The egress proxy blocks Lever, Greenhouse, Jobvite, Built In, LinkedIn, Rock Health, Fierce Healthcare and fintech.global.
