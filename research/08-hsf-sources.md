## Research limitations, stated plainly

**Nothing here was fetched directly.** The environment's egress proxy blocked every one of these domains on direct fetch: `jobs.lever.co`, `job-boards.greenhouse.io`, `boards.greenhouse.io`, `jobs.jobvite.com`, `builtin.com`, `linkedin.com`, `rockhealth.com`, `fiercehealthcare.com`, `fintech.global` and `fintechfutures.com`. Everything in this list came through search indexing, which returns accurate quoted text but cannot confirm a posting is still open. **Verify before you reference a posting by name.**

**No LinkedIn profile was opened.** No individual is named anywhere in `06-healthcare-saas-fintech.md`. Where a person is needed, the list gives the role to target and leaves the name to you.

**No email address appears in either document.** None was found published for these companies, and none was constructed from a pattern. The reason has not changed: a bounced cold email damages your sending domain's reputation for months, and a guessed address bounces most of the time.

**Funding figures are single-sourced.** Each round in Tier 4 rests on one trade publication. The amounts are as reported and were not cross-checked against a second source or a regulatory filing.

**Tier 2's weakest four rows are exactly that.** Lumanity, Nucleus Holdings, Bioscript Group and Aptitude Health appear in 2026 agency roundups and nothing more. They are on the list because the category is right, not because anything specific was found. Graded Weak for that reason.

**Compliance, unchanged from `05-email-campaign.md`.** Germany and Austria are opt-in jurisdictions, so fino data services and Enzo should be approached on LinkedIn rather than by email. Turn off open and click tracking for UK and EU recipients. Never fake a `Re:` in a subject line.
