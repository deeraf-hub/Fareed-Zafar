## Research limitations — stated plainly

1. **Several major news domains were blocked by this environment's network egress policy** (TechCrunch, Crunchbase News, FinSMEs, Tech.eu, EU-Startups, Techstars.com and others could not be fetched directly). Their content was reached through search-engine indexing instead. The links above are correct and are worth opening directly to confirm details before you use them.
2. **No individual LinkedIn profile was verified.** Named people appear only where a public source names them. Everywhere else the list gives a **job title to target**, which you'll resolve in seconds on LinkedIn.
3. **Job postings are perishable.** Segment A and parts of F rest on postings live 10–18 Aug 2026. Confirm status before referencing one.
4. **A few Segment C entries are thin** — Endra and Osmo Studio in particular have a confirmed round but limited public detail. They're flagged "verify details before contact" in the CSV. Do that before you send.
