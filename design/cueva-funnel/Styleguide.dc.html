00  —  Where This Comes From
Ground
A deep, desaturated dark carries the brand; a single pale cool tint carries everything else. Taken from the supplied guidelines, where the dark green-slate and mint-sage pairing does all the work.
Line Work
Tone-on-tone concentric arcs, drawn large and cropped by the frame. The signature device in both supplied systems, and here it reads as an arch — a cueva, a shelter.
Restraint
Zero corner radius, hairline rules instead of boxes, one accent, and a serif reserved for headlines. Premium comes from what is left out.
