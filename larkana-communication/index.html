How does paying through mobile top-ups work?

Select your network (Jazz, Zong, Telenor or Ufone) in the top-up section, enter your number and amount, and continue on WhatsApp. Our team will share the exact steps and confirm your order once the top-up is received.

Do you deliver outside Karachi?

Yes. We ship from Karachi to cities across Pakistan. Delivery time and charges depend on your location and are confirmed when you place your order.

Are your products original?

We stock quality accessories and clearly list the brand and specifications of each product. If you have a question about a specific item, just ask before ordering.

What if I receive a faulty item?

Contact us with your order details and a photo or video of the issue. We'll arrange a replacement according to our replacement policy.

What are your business hours?

We're available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM. Messages sent outside these hours are answered on the next working day.
