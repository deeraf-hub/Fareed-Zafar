Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to collect a money transfer?

Your transaction reference number (MTCN / PIN / tracking number) from the sender, plus your original CNIC or passport. The name on the transfer must match your ID exactly.

How long does a transfer take to arrive?

Most transfers through major networks are available within minutes of being sent. Bank-to-bank transfers can take 1–2 working days depending on the banks involved.

Are your exchange rates negotiable for large amounts?

Yes — for larger currency exchange transactions we can often offer an improved rate. Call or WhatsApp us with the amount and we'll quote you directly.

Is there a limit on how much I can receive?

Limits depend on the transfer network and current regulations. For amounts above standard limits, additional documentation may be required — contact us and we'll guide you through it.

Do you charge a fee to receive money?

No — receiving a remittance payout is free. The sender pays the transfer fee on their side. What we quote is what you get.
