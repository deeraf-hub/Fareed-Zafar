Questions

What shippers ask us first

How long does transit take?

Karachi to Dubai runs 2 days door to door by air. Karachi to New York is 5 to 7 days by air and 28 to 34 days by sea. Ground moves inside Pakistan take 1 to 3 days. Your quote carries the exact cut-off and delivery date for your lane.

Is my cargo insured?

Carrier liability is capped by convention, at roughly USD 20 per kilo by air and USD 700 per package by sea, which rarely covers the goods. We add all-risk marine cover at 0.4 percent of declared value, invoiced with the freight. Declare the commercial value on the booking, not the cost price.

What will you not carry?

No currency, live animals, narcotics, firearms or ammunition. Lithium batteries, paints, aerosols and perfumes move as dangerous goods, and they need a shipper declaration, certified packing and 3 extra days of lead time.

How does payment work?

New accounts pay before departure by bank transfer in PKR or USD. After three cleared shipments you can apply for 30-day credit, subject to two trade references. Duties and taxes are billed at cost with the receipt attached.

What happens if cargo is damaged?

Write the damage on the delivery receipt before you sign, then send photos within 3 days. We file with the carrier and the insurer, and we give you a claim reference and an expected settlement date within 5 business days.
