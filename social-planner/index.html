🔌 Connecting Facebook, Instagram & LinkedIn

Let me be straight with you, so you don't waste money or time. There are three honest ways to get posts onto the accounts. Pick one.

Way 1 — Plan here, post with one click (works today, free)

You plan the full month in this planner. On the day, open the post, press Copy — the caption and hashtags go to your clipboard — then press the Facebook / Instagram / LinkedIn button and paste. Takes about 20 seconds per post. Nothing to set up, nothing to pay, no account can get blocked.

Way 2 — Plan here, schedule free in Meta Business Suite (recommended)

Facebook and Instagram give you a free official scheduler. You plan and get approval here, then once a month you paste the approved captions into Meta Business Suite and it posts automatically at the right time. LinkedIn also has a built-in scheduler (the little clock icon under the post box) — also free.

Open Meta Business Suite ↗ Open LinkedIn ↗
Way 3 — Fully automatic posting (needs a server, one-time setup)

For the planner to post by itself while your laptop is closed, something must stay switched on 24/7. A single HTML file cannot do that — the browser is not allowed to talk to Facebook or LinkedIn directly, and even if it were, it would need secret keys that must never sit inside a file people can open.

So the planner has a hand-over point instead: give it one web address (a "webhook") and it will send the approved posts there. That address can be an automation tool you already know — n8n, Make, or Zapier — which then does the actual posting. You do this setup once, and it also happens to be exactly what the Messenger→WhatsApp service in this same repository already does, so the hard part is a road you have walked before.

💾 Backup & Restore

Everything you type is saved inside this browser on this computer. It does not go to the internet and nobody else can see it. But if you clear your browser data or change computer, it is gone — so download a backup once a week. It takes one click.

Want the same plan on your phone and laptop? Put this file in your Google Drive or Dropbox folder and download the backup file into the same folder — then restore it on the other device.

❓ How to use this — in 5 minutes

Step 1 — Add your clients

Go to Clients and add each client once: their name, their Facebook page, Instagram handle and LinkedIn page. Give each one a colour so you can spot them on the calendar instantly.

Step 2 — Plan the whole month at once

Press the green Plan Whole Month button. Choose the client, tick which days of the week you post on, set the time, and paste all your captions — one caption per line. Press Create, and your entire month appears on the calendar in one go.

Step 3 — Get client approval

Press Print / PDF and send the clean month sheet to your client on WhatsApp or email. When they say yes, mark the posts Approved (green).

Step 4 — Post

On the day, open the post, press Copy, then press the Facebook / Instagram / LinkedIn button, and paste. Then mark it Posted. Or schedule them all in Meta Business Suite in one sitting — see the Connections tab.

Step 5 — Back it up

Once a week, go to Backup and press Download. That's your safety net.

Small things that save you time
  • Click any empty day on the calendar to add a post straight to that day.
  • Drag a post from one day to another to move it.
  • Press Copy this post on any post to duplicate it for a different client.
  • The colour bars at the top of the calendar tell you how many posts each client has this month.
  • Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your clients' data stays on your computer.
