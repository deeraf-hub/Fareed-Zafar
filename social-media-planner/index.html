Fill the whole month in one go

Choose a client, pick the days they post on, and empty posts will be made for the full month. Then you just open each one and write the caption.

How to use this (2 minutes)

1. Add your clients. Left side, click + Add. Give the name, pick a colour, paste their Facebook / Instagram / LinkedIn handles.

2. Plan the month fast. Click Fill Whole Month at the top. Pick the client, tick the days (like Mon, Wed, Fri), choose the time. It creates all the posts for that month at once.

3. Write the posts. Click any post on the calendar to open it. Write caption, hashtags, paste the design link, add a note for your designer. Save.

4. Move a post. Just drag it from one date and drop it on another date.

5. Mark progress. Every post is Draft → then Approved (client said yes) → then Posted (it is live). Colours on the calendar show you where things stand.

6. Send to client. Click Print / PDF and choose "Save as PDF" — one clean page you can email or WhatsApp. Or click Excel to get a sheet.

7. Keep it safe. Your work saves by itself in this browser. Click Save Backup once a week to keep a file on your computer. On a new laptop, click Open Backup and everything comes back.

Note: this is a planner, not an auto-poster. It tells you exactly what to post and when — you still post it from the client's own account (or paste it into Meta Business Suite).
